# Demo scenario: Founder Arrival

A deterministic, presenter-driven run of the real ADAPT pipeline for a live hackathon
demonstration. Nothing in it is canned: synthetic documents are read by the real document
reader, the plan comes from the cited governance graph, research comes from the curated
source list, and the what-if re-runs the real simulation. It is repeatable because every
input is fixed and every capability runs on its deterministic engine (ARCHITECTURE.md D25 to
D28).

Open it at **`/demo/founder-arrival`** (sidebar: "Founder Arrival demo"; Settings on phones).
It appears when the backend reports the `demo_scenarios` feature, which follows
`DEMO_AUTH_ENABLED` and is therefore never on in production. Rehearse it headlessly with
`cd backend && uv run python scripts/demo_founder_arrival.py --twice`.

## 1. The persona (fictional)

| | |
|---|---|
| Founder | Kabir Rahman, Indian national, born 18 Jul 1991 |
| Spouse | Ayesha Rahman, born 14 Mar 1994, moving with him |
| Company | Noorvia Labs Ltd, a software and AI start-up planned in ADGM |
| Goals | Set up the company, residence visa, a home, sponsor his spouse |
| Languages | English, Hindi |
| Community | Asks for Indian community recommendations (community consent) and, voluntarily, Muslim community and faith resources (faith consent plus a stated faith) |
| Not given | His monthly income: ADAPT has to ask rather than guess |

Nothing is inferred. Nationality comes from the passport. The spouse and her date of birth
come from the marriage certificate, and the company and its jurisdiction from the business
profile. Faith comes only from his own statement with opt-in. Kabir confirms what ADAPT read
from each document (`POST /documents/{id}/review`). That confirmation is what lets his
nationality personalise community research: research uses background only when the person
provided or confirmed it.

## 2. Synthetic documents

Generated by `app/demo_scenarios/documents.py` as real one-page PDFs with a text layer. Each
carries a red "SPECIMEN - SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT" band, a diagonal watermark, a "fictional"
footer, a fake passport number and no real emblem. They are written to
`demo-documents/founder-arrival/` (regenerate with
`uv run python -m app.demo_scenarios.documents ../demo-documents/founder-arrival`) and served
to the page by the scenario kit. Every value is printed unambiguously (day above 12, month
names, explicit yes/no words), so every field reads back at or above the 0.85 review
threshold and no correction gate opens.

| Document | What the reader extracts (local PDF text layer) | What it changes in the plan |
|---|---|---|
| Passport | name, nationality, date of birth, expiry; MRZ check digits verified, so every field is at 98% | Nationality personalises community research; `documents.passport` is satisfied |
| Marriage certificate | the spouse and her date of birth, marriage date and country, registrar, **home-country attestation: yes**, **UAE attestation: no** (90%) | Adds the family chain. Her age settles the spouse's medical-certificate condition. India's attestation is done, so the chain starts at the UAE mission visit, which is the one appointment ready now |
| Business profile | company name, legal form, planned jurisdiction ADGM, activities; stage `idea`, and no issuer (no authority issued a plan) | ADGM incorporation instead of an assumed mainland licence, so no assumption risk |

## 3. The sequence

| Act | Public routes | What the audience sees |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh start | logout, reload, then `POST /auth/demo-session` | a new private account every run |
| 1. Meet the founder | `POST /onboarding/profile` (consents included) | the persona, each fact tagged Stated, From a document or Not known |
| 2. Documents | `POST /documents/upload` one at a time, each awaited, then `POST /documents/{id}/review` | per document, the synthetic PDF beside **Document uploaded, Document analyzed, Facts extracted, User graph updated**, lit from the reading run's real events (`extract`, `structure`, `update_twin`) with their durations; then the fields with the reader's confidence and method, "Confirmed by Kabir", and his private twin with document-read entities ringed |
| 3. Settlement graph | `POST /journey {deterministic: true}` | the agent's twelve stages live; then 22 steps and 32 dependencies in area bands by dependency depth, featured roles tagged (Company, Residence, Document, Family, Appointment, Official handoff), the family chain highlighted, the **missing-information blocker** (Sponsor's minimum income) with an optional live answer, and the evidence behind each major requirement (trust tier, quoted passage, official source, last checked) |
| 4. Things you may not have considered | `GET /journey/{id}` (`considerations`) | two cited findings: the next attestation stamp is added in the issuing country, before you fly; the spouse's visa waits on a lease registered in Tawtheeq, which itself waits on your own visa and Emirates ID |
| 5. Background research | `POST /research {journey_id, mode: "snapshot"}` | progress, then Indian community (Embassy of India, ISC, Kerala Social Centre), Muslim community and faith (SZGMC prayer times, Awqaf, u.ae Islamic affairs, SZGMC), start-up and professional (ADGM, IBPG, Hub71, ...), cultural guidance and starter services (TAMM first). Every card shows its source URL, retrieval date and "ADAPT reviewed source list" |
| 6. What if: move alone first | `POST /journey/{id}/simulate {household.move_with_spouse: false}` | 11 steps leave the first-arrival plan, 15 dependencies disappear (drawn dashed on the same chain), both findings move to Ayesha's later phase; the base plan is untouched |
| 7. Action cards | `GET /actions` | **Approval required** and **Demo adapter** on the UAE mission visit (example slots, nothing booked), **Approval required** on the sponsor file (TAMM, UAE PASS), **Prepared** and **Official handoff** on the two official-page steps. Nothing says submitted |

Featured roles come from the scenario definition and are matched against the real plan:
company `service.company_registration_adgm`, residence `service.residence_visa_investor`,
document `service.mofa_attestation`, family `service.family_residence_visa@spouse`,
appointment `appointment.uae_mission_visit`, official handoff `service.sponsor_file`, and the
missing-information blocker on the family visa.

## 4. Determinism

* Inputs are constants in `app/demo_scenarios/founder_arrival.py`. The page and the rehearsal
  script read the same definition from `GET /api/demo/scenarios/founder-arrival` and post its
  bodies verbatim.
* Documents are uploaded one at a time, each after the previous has settled (the marriage
  certificate needs the passport's name to tell which party is the spouse).
* `deterministic: true` on the journey uses the rule-based intake parser and drafting
  templates even with an LLM key, and its research and what-ifs follow. `mode: "snapshot"`
  pins research to the curated list, and a journey reuses that job rather than starting
  another.
* No arrival date is given, so nothing is computed relative to today except the demo adapter's
  example appointment slots (labelled as examples, never booked).
* The rehearsal prints a fingerprint: document fields, plan steps, edges and blockers,
  findings, research URLs per group, the what-if diff and the action cards. `--twice` asserts
  that two fresh runs match. Verified on 2026-10-02: three fresh runs gave identical
  fingerprints.

## 5. Honesty rules the demo keeps

* The documents say SPECIMEN on every page. The reader extracts what is printed, and nothing
  is pre-filled; upload a different file and you get a different result.
* Stage animations replay the reading run's own events, in order, at a readable pace. Each
  stage shows its real summary and duration.
* Research results say how they were retrieved ("ADAPT reviewed source list: a person checked
  this page on {date}. Not a live web search.").
* Actions stop at `handoff_required`. Demo adapters show example slots and a completeness
  check marked **Demo adapter**; approving one hands off to the official site and books or
  submits nothing. The card vocabulary (`lib/actionCard.ts`) has no "Submitted". Elsewhere,
  submitted and completed only ever appear with their source (the person's confirmation or
  an external reference).
* A finding without an official citation is not shown.

## 6. Presenter runbook

1. `docker compose up -d --build` (no OpenAI key needed; with one, the scenario stays
   deterministic).
2. Rehearse: `cd backend && uv run python scripts/demo_founder_arrival.py --twice`. It ends with
   "READY FOR THE STAGE".
3. Open `http://localhost:8080/demo/founder-arrival` and choose **Start a fresh run**.
4. Step through the acts with **Continue**, or **Play all** (pause any time). Each finished
   act links to the full screen (Documents, Agent run, Discover, What if, Approvals).
5. Optional live moments:
   * hover a role tag to light up its whole chain;
   * type an income into the blocker's question to clear it;
   * approve the UAE mission appointment and watch it become an **Official handoff**, not
     "booked".
6. The synthetic PDFs render in desktop browsers' built-in PDF viewer. Headless browsers show
   an empty frame; use "Open the synthetic PDF" there.
